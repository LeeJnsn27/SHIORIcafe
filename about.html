The Story of SHIORI

SHIORI began as a single shelf of secondhand books and one coffee machine, tucked into the corner of a friend's bookstore. Students kept asking to stay "just one more hour," so eventually we built a room for exactly that: a place with no closing bell on your concentration.

Today SHIORI is still small on purpose. We would rather have twenty quiet desks than two hundred noisy ones. Every nook is named, every drink is unhurried, and every visitor is welcome to stay until their thoughts feel finished.
